# SHARING AND FEEDBACK

Throughout the meaningful **First Cloud AI Journey** internship, I would like to share my personal opinions and feedback below to help improve the program for future cohorts:

## General Program Evaluation

**1. Learning and Working Environment**
The program is organized very professionally and methodically. The learning guide system is clear, and the detailed roadmap helps interns easily track their personal progress week by week. The combination of self-study and workshops maximizes flexibility.

**2. Support from Mentors & Admin Team**
The mentor team is highly experienced and enthusiastic. Whenever I faced difficulties in network configuration, backend processing algorithm optimization, or system debugging, the mentors always guided me through the problem-solving mindset instead of giving ready-made answers, significantly improving my technical thinking. The admin team also provided technical support and issued AWS practice accounts very quickly.

**3. Alignment with Major**
As a **Computer Science** major, I found the practical exercises very close to industry reality. Building event-driven systems, optimizing resource configurations (RAM, Timeout) for Lambda functions, establishing serverless architectures, and managing NoSQL databases helped me deeply understand how to realize algorithmic models and distributed system architectures on the cloud computing platform.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**
The program is a great opportunity to access modern cloud technologies such as Serverless (Lambda, S3, DynamoDB), Containers, and modern infrastructure. In addition to professional skills in programming and system optimization, I also improved my technical writing, time management, and system design thinking.

**5. Culture & Teamwork Spirit**
The knowledge-sharing culture at the AWS Study Group is very vibrant. Everyone is open, unafraid to ask questions, and actively helps each other overcome complex labs.

---

## Survey Responses

* **What were you most satisfied with during your internship?**
  It was the opportunity to access and have hands-on practice with actual cloud services, helping to transform theoretical knowledge of data structures, algorithms, and operating systems into a complete technology product running on a production environment.

* **What do you think the program needs to improve for future cohorts?**
  Weekly recurring small group check-in discussions should be added so interns can interact directly and share code optimizations or common error handling encountered during system building.

* **Would you recommend this program to your friends?**
  Definitely yes. This is a well-structured cloud practice program that helps Computer Science students shorten the gap between academic theory and real-world corporate products.

---

## Proposals & Aspirations

* **Proposals:** The organizers could design additional Deep Dive sessions on distributed systems design and automated deployment workflows (CI/CD) on the cloud to give Computer Science students more practical perspectives on performance optimization.
* **Aspirations:** I hope to continue accompanying and participating in the community activities of the AWS Study Group in the future to keep updating new technology trends.
