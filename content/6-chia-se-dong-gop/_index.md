# SHARING & FEEDBACK

After a meaningful **First Cloud AI Journey** internship, I would like to share my personal opinions and feedback below to contribute to improving the program for future cohorts:

## General Evaluation of the Program

**1. Learning and Working Environment**
The program is organized very professionally and systematically. The study guide documentation is clear, and the detailed roadmap helps interns easily track their progress week by week. The combination of self-study and workshops maximizes flexibility.

**2. Support from Mentors & Admin Team**
The mentor team is highly experienced and enthusiastic. When I faced difficulties configuring secure networks or debugging CI/CD, the mentors always guided me on how to think and check system logs rather than giving ready-made answers, which helped me improve my problem-solving skills. The Admin board also provided technical support and issued AWS Free Tier practice accounts very quickly.

**3. Relevance to My Major**
As a **Cybersecurity** major at HUTECH University, I found the practical content very close to industry reality. Manually configuring VPC Isolation, Public/Private Subnets, setting up Security Groups, Network ACLs, and applying Least Privilege control with IAM Policies helped me deeply understand how to secure cloud infrastructure.

**4. Learning Opportunities & Skill Development**
The program is a great opportunity for me to approach modern cloud technologies such as Serverless (Lambda, API Gateway), Containers (Docker, ECS), and Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation). In addition to professional skills, I also improved my technical report writing and time management skills.

**5. Culture & Team Spirit**
The knowledge-sharing culture at the AWS Study Group is very active. Everyone is open-minded, not afraid to ask questions, and actively helps each other overcome difficult labs.

---

## Survey Questionnaire Answers

*   **What are you most satisfied with during the internship?**
    It was being provided with an AWS account for hands-on practice on real cloud services, which helped turn abstract theoretical knowledge into actual running products.

*   **What do you think the program needs to improve for future cohorts?**
    There should be some regular weekly small group discussions (study group check-ins) so that interns can interact directly and share common configuration errors.

*   **Would you recommend this program to your friends?**
    Definitely. This is the most systematic cloud practice program that helps students bridge the gap between university and the enterprise.

## Proposals & Wishes

*   **Proposals:** The organizers could design additional Deep Dive sessions on Microservices architecture and deployment automation (CI/CD) on the cloud so that Computer Science students can gain more practical perspectives.
*   **Wishes:** I wish to continue accompanying and participating in the AWS Study Group's community activities in the future to keep updating myself with new technologies.
